# Packages & Customization

All engagements are customized based on business goals and requirements. Exact inclusions, exclusions, revision rounds, and pricing are confirmed with the team during the discovery/strategy stage — they are not fixed in this document.

To get accurate numbers for a specific project, the team prepares a custom quotation after a short discovery conversation.

# How We Work (Workflow)

1. **Discovery Meeting** — We understand your business, goals, target audience, and project requirements.
2. **Strategy** — We create a customized marketing strategy tailored to your objectives.
3. **Planning** — We prepare the content plan, timeline, and execution roadmap.
4. **Design** — Our creative team develops branding, designs, and visual content.
5. **Revisions** — We review the work with you and apply the agreed revisions.
6. **Production** — We produce the final content, videos, websites, or marketing assets.
7. **Launch** — We publish and launch your campaign across the selected platforms.
8. **Monthly Reports** — We monitor performance, analyze results, and optimize future campaigns.

# Company Policies

## Payment Policy
A deposit is required before starting any project, with the remaining balance paid according to the agreed payment schedule.

## Refund Policy
Payments made for completed work are non-refundable. Refund eligibility depends on the project stage.

## Revision Policy
Each project includes a predefined number of revision rounds according to the selected package. Additional revisions may incur extra charges.

## Project Timeline
Project timelines are agreed upon before work begins and may vary depending on project scope and client feedback.

## Support
Technical support and consultation are available throughout the project, with additional support options available after delivery if required.

## Ownership Rights
Final approved designs and deliverables become the client's property after full payment. Apex Creative reserves the right to showcase completed work in its portfolio unless otherwise agreed.
